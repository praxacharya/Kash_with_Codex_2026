# Market Catalyst Tracker — 2026-11-18 (window: 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-28) — Correction: Huang resigned Nov 12

Major correction to this tracker's prior reporting: Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's President, CEO, and board member effective immediately on Nov 12, 2026 (confirmed via Nvidia's SEC 8-K, "NVIDIA Announces CEO Transition") — not merely under investigation while remaining CEO, as earlier (Nov 8-9-dated) company statements had indicated. CFO Colette Kress was named interim CEO while retaining her CFO role; Harvey C. Jones (former Synopsys CEO) was named Nvidia's first-ever board chair. The stock reportedly fell as much as 10% further on the resignation news. Nvidia's fiscal Q3 2027 earnings still went ahead as scheduled on Nov 17 under Kress, but the actual results and stock reaction could not be confirmed in available sources as of this scan — pre-earnings consensus was EPS $2.47 (+90% YoY) on ~$108.7-108.8B revenue. Succession speculation (Arm's Rene Haas, AMD's Lisa Su, Intel's Lip-Bu Tan, Eric Schmidt, Bret Taylor, even Tim Cook per a Trump suggestion) remains unresolved — no permanent CEO has been named. Today's Nov 18 House briefing status is unclear given the resignation; one report says Huang separately agreed to testify before Congress before Thanksgiving as a private citizen/former CEO.
