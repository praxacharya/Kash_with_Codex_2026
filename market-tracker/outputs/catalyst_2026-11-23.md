# Market Catalyst Tracker — 2026-11-23 (window: 2026-11-23 to 2026-12-03)

**Correction to prior tracking:** The Russia secondary-sanctions/tariff thread carried in this tracker since Oct 18 as "effective, enforcement unconfirmed" needs to be corrected and retired. Trump actually signed an executive order on **Oct 16, 2026** — two days *before* the Graham Act's Russia provisions were due to take effect — waiving nearly all of them, including the 500% secondary-tariff authority and the requirement to sanction foreign banks doing significant business with Russian state banks. The provisions never actually took effect; there was never going to be an enforcement action to find. The waiver is tied to a Russia-brokered diesel-supply deal and is reversible by the president at will. Separately: Nvidia's board has named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO (Harvey Jones is board chair) while the permanent search continues — Rene Haas (Arm) and Lisa Su (AMD) remain the most-cited outside names, now joined by newly floated names Tim Cook (publicly suggested by Trump), Google Cloud's Thomas Kurian, and Micron's Sanjay Mehrotra; reporting also says Huang is quietly involved in the search and has contacted at least one candidate, in tension with the board's public line that he won't return. Oil has been volatile and mixed since mid-November rather than cleanly extending the early-November decline. US markets are closed Nov 26 (Thanksgiving) and close early Nov 27.
