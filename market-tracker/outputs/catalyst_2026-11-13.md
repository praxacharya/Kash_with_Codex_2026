Separately, chip stocks had bounced Nov 6 (SOXX +~5% premarket) after the correction, partly attributed to AI-datacenter-policy concerns tied to the Democratic Congress win; Texas also froze new data-center permits pending a grid-risk audit (Nov 3). On Iran/oil, no newer confirmed data than the Nov 2/9-10 diplomatic-progress reporting from previous days; still unresolved whether a formal deal follows.
